Ongoing projects

  • Variable-Stiffness Suspension and Vibration-Aware Control for a Rough-Terrain Rover ↗

    Field robotics · Suspension and control · Sep 2026 — Present

    Rough ground shakes the cameras and sensors on an inspection rover. We designed a six-wheel rover in CAD with adjustable spring preload at each wheel to see whether we could keep the body steadier without slowing down.

  • Combat Robot Development ↗

    Combat robotics

    At NITK Battle Bots, we designed a 3 lb flipper inspired by Jelly Baby and built GN4RLY 2.5, a 15 kg hammer saw. We used a one-piece chassis on the small bot to cut down on parts. On the bigger bot, we had to stay within the weight limit while leaving room to reach parts for repairs.

  • Tensegrity-Based Variable-Stiffness Knee Joint for a Knee Exoskeleton ↗

    Wearable robotics · May 2026 — Present

    I wanted to see if tendon tension could make a wearable knee joint more or less stiff. In my design, 12 tendons hold a three-armed piece between two rigid frames. I’m using MATLAB to check how much tension the tendons need to stay taut under load.

  • Terrain-Adaptive Leaf Cleaning Module ↗

    Mechanical design and prototyping · Jul 2026 — Present

    Wet leaves sit close to the ground, especially in dips. We designed a pickup module with six units that can move up and down on their own so the pickup can follow uneven ground. We tried one 3D-printed unit outdoors.

Past projects

  • Physics-Informed Multi-Fidelity FEM Surrogate for Reaction-Wheel-Induced Spacecraft Microvibration and Payload Jitter ↗

    Spacecraft dynamics · Physics-informed machine learning · Aug — Oct 2026

    I built a faster way to model how vibrations from four reaction wheels reach a spacecraft camera. A stiffer mount looked promising in the first pass, but a more detailed simulation showed so little improvement that I kept the original stiffness.

  • Actuated Knee-Assistance System ↗

    Assistive mechatronics · Oct 2025 — Sep 2026

    We started with a spring that helps straighten the knee and turned it into a brace that uses a motor and cable to adjust the assistance. The design placed in the top five at the Incubate X Prosthetic Challenge.

  • UAV Vibration Analysis & Isolation ↗

    UAV hardware · Jun — Jul 2026

    At Vayu Aerospace, I looked at how motor vibration reaches the sensor in a UAV’s flight controller. I compared three mounting designs, and ground tests helped us choose a tray with rubber-like isolators.

  • Uncertainty-Aware Navigation for an Indoor Mobile Robot ↗

    State estimation & autonomy · Sep — Dec 2025

    I wanted to see what a robot should do when its wheels slip and it’s no longer sure where it is. In simulated runs with wheel slip, slowing down when the position estimate became uncertain helped the robot finish more runs and hit fewer obstacles.

  • Exploring the Fireworks Galaxy ↗

    Astronomy · Image analysis

    I started with a Hubble image at my school’s astronomy club and zoomed in on the bright centre of the Fireworks Galaxy. I tried two filters and checked which details were also visible in the original image.

  • Four-Bar Linkage Door-Opening Mechanism ↗

    Kinematics & fabrication · Feb — Apr 2024

    Turn a crank and the door swings open. We built a working prototype to see how four connected links could make that happen. We also used CAD to compare two ways of opening a pair of lids.

  • Hands-free Cursor Control ↗

    Python · Computer vision · 2023

    I built a Python prototype to move the cursor without a mouse. It uses a webcam to follow a fingertip or track where I’m looking. I added smoothing in hand mode so small shifts in tracking wouldn’t make the cursor jump.

  • Brain Tumour Detection ↗

    Python · Machine learning · 2021

    We prepared and augmented brain MRI scans with and without tumours, then trained a convolutional neural network to tell the two groups apart. We placed second among more than 100 teams from the region at the 2022 NITK EngiConnect Project Expo.
