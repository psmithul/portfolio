Ongoing projects

  • Variable-Stiffness Suspension and Vibration-Aware Control for a Rough-Terrain Rover ↗

    Field robotics · Suspension and control · Sep 2026 — Present

    We designed a six-wheel inspection rover with a motor that can adjust the spring preload at each wheel. The goal is to keep the rover’s body steadier over rough ground at the same speed.

  • Combat Robot Development ↗

    Combat robotics

    At NITK Battle Bots, we designed a 3 lb flipper with a one-piece chassis and built the 15 kg GN4RLY 2.5. The two builds taught me how packaging, fabrication, and repair access affect a team at an event.

  • Tensegrity-Based Variable-Stiffness Knee Joint for a Knee Exoskeleton ↗

    Tensegrity · Knee exoskeletons · May 2026 — Present

    This knee joint uses 12 tendons to suspend a three-armed piece between two rigid frames. I’m using MATLAB to find out how much initial tension the tendons need to stay taut under load.

  • Terrain-Adaptive Leaf Cleaning Module ↗

    Cleaning module · Mechanical design · Jul 2026 — Present

    We designed a cleaning module for wet and dry leaves on uneven ground. Six identical tiller units use telescoping shafts and roller feet to help the pickup follow the surface.

Past projects

  • Physics-Informed Multi-Fidelity FEM Surrogate for Reaction-Wheel-Induced Spacecraft Microvibration and Payload Jitter ↗

    Spacecraft dynamics · Physics-informed machine learning · Aug — Oct 2026

    I built a faster model of how vibration from four reaction wheels reaches a spacecraft camera. I used it to test whether a stiffer mount would improve the simulated pointing response over 5–20 Hz.

  • Actuated Knee-Assistance System ↗

    Assistive mechatronics · Oct 2025 — Sep 2026

    We developed a passive spring mechanism into a brace that uses a motor and cable to assist knee extension. Our team placed in the top five at the Incubate X Prosthetic Challenge.

  • UAV Vibration Analysis & Isolation ↗

    UAV hardware · Jun — Jul 2026

    I compared three ways to mount a UAV flight controller at Vayu Aerospace. Ground motor tests helped us choose an elastomer-isolated tray.

  • Uncertainty-Aware Navigation for an Indoor Mobile Robot ↗

    State estimation & autonomy · Sep — Dec 2025

    I built a robot simulator to see how a robot should react when wheel slip or noisy sensors make its position uncertain. I tested when it would slow down or try to recover.

  • Exploring the Fireworks Galaxy ↗

    Astronomy · Image analysis

    I started this project at school to explore the bright centre of NGC 6946 in a near-infrared Hubble image. I worked from the full detector frame down to the nucleus and compared what two ridge filters brought out.

  • Four-Bar Linkage Door-Opening Mechanism ↗

    Kinematics & fabrication · Feb — Apr 2024

    We built a four-bar mechanism that opens a door. We chose the lengths and pivots of the links around the motion we wanted the door to follow.

  • Hands-free Cursor Control ↗

    Python · Computer vision · 2023

    I built a Python prototype that processed camera frames in real time and translated hand gestures and eye movements into cursor movement.

  • Brain Tumour Detection ↗

    Python · Machine learning · 2021

    We trained a CNN to detect whether a brain MRI image contains a tumour. Our team placed second among more than 100 teams from the region at the 2022 NITK EngiConnect Project Expo.
