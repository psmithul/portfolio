Dayfold

Personal software project

A study desk for planning work, reading papers, and keeping research notes connected to the pages they came from.

Dayfold has personal and shared desks. Its local preview stores notes and documents on the device.

Why I built it

I wanted a place to study where a paper, the notes I took while reading it, and the work that came out of it could stay connected. A note is more useful to me when I can find the page it came from and the project it belongs to.

I also wanted the same space to work when studying alone or with someone else. That shaped the decision to keep a personal desk and a shared desk inside one workspace.

Reading and taking notes

I grouped the main actions around the paper itself. I can mark a passage in a PDF, keep a handwritten note alongside it, and record a takeaway that leads back to the source page. Linking a follow-up task to the same project makes it easier to return to the reading with its context intact.

The daybook brings dated tasks and time blocks into one view. I kept that daily view separate from the paper library so the reading material stays organized even when the plan for the day changes.

Working together

I built personal and shared desks so two people can work on the same plan without mixing their private notes. The shared desk supports notes and tasks both people can edit, and the study session lets them follow a common focus timer from their own devices.

The local preview stores records and documents on the device. I used that preview to work on the reading and planning flows with storage separate from the hosted workspace.

What I learned

Designing the paper view taught me to follow a note through its whole life: where it is taken, what source it points to, and how it is found again. The link to the page matters most when I return to the work later.

Working through the personal and shared desks taught me that collaboration needs a clear boundary. The shared timer and desk are useful because it is easy to tell which work belongs to both people.
