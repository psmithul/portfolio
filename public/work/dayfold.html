Dayfold

Personal software project

I built Dayfold because I wanted my papers, reading notes, and project tasks in one place. I wanted to be able to come back to a note, find the page it came from, and see what I was working on at the time.

Paper library and daybook

I organised the main reading actions around the PDF. I can highlight a passage, write a note by hand, and record a takeaway that links to the source page. I can connect a follow-up task to the same project. Working through the process from taking a note to reopening it made the page link an important part of the design.

I kept the original PDF available and made annotated copies exportable. The research notebook can still link to a page in the original, and an annotated copy can be read outside Dayfold.

The daybook shows dated tasks and time blocks. I kept it separate from the paper library because the schedule changes daily, while the papers and their annotations need a stable place.

The practical test for the layout was whether I could open a takeaway later and find the passage again. I treated an annotation as something that belongs on a page and a takeaway as something I might want to read on its own. Keeping the page link on the takeaway lets me return to the source even when I am working from a project or a dated entry.

Personal and shared desks

I made a personal desk for each person and a shared desk for work both people can edit. A study session has a common timer that each person can follow from their own device. That gives a shared study session a place without exposing personal notes.

The local preview stores its records and documents on the device. I used it to work on the reading and planning flows with storage separate from the hosted workspace.

The local preview gave me a way to work through the sequence from opening a paper to returning to a saved note. The shared desk has a different check: two people need to agree on the timer and the material they can edit, while each person’s notes stay in their own desk. A single-device preview only covers the personal side of that design.
