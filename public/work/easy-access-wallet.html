Wallet for Easier Card Access

Design project · Sep — Nov 2023

I designed a wallet concept around the motion of taking one card from a tight stack. I wanted to leave space for a finger while keeping the rest of the stack supported.

Card removal

I drew the action as three movements: reaching the stack, getting a fingertip under an edge, and lifting the card far enough to take hold of it. Looking at the fingertip and the card edge gave me a specific place to change the wallet’s shape.

Pocket geometry

I left an opening at the point where the finger reaches the edge and kept support around the rest of the stack. In the schematic I drew the opening and card stack together so I could judge which parts of the pocket still needed to be there.
