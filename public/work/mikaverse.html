Mikaverse

Personal game project

A Minecraft Java sandbox built around exploring settlements, travelling by rail, and bringing discoveries back to a shared home.

The project runs as a Fabric-based world for small-group play.

What I wanted exploration to feel like

I wanted a Minecraft world where setting out from home had a purpose. A tower on a ridge or a rail line disappearing into the distance can give players a reason to pick a direction before they know what is there.

I built Mikaverse as a Fabric-based Minecraft Java sandbox for a small group. I used travel and a shared home as the structure for the experience. Players can spend time exploring separately and still bring something useful back to the group.

Making places worth visiting

Railways, towers, settlements, and old inscriptions give players reasons to leave home and see what is further down the line.

I used physical clues to connect the places. A settlement can provide context for a nearby inscription, and an inscription can reveal a story about the people who lived there. The archive mural shown above is one example of a discovery built into the environment.

The same signs keep their meanings across inscriptions. Recognizing a sign from an earlier trip can help a player read a new inscription, which connects discoveries made in different places.

Bringing things back

Discoveries have a place in the shared home. A rubbing of a script or a book can become part of a library the group builds over time.

A rubbing lets a player carry a copy of an inscription and compare it with other finds at home. Books and rubbings work as items the group can keep together in the library, giving a trip an outcome other players can see.

What I learned

Designing the inscriptions and rubbings taught me to think through the whole trip. The clue has to be visible enough to draw someone in, the discovery needs a reason to matter, and the item brought home needs a place in the shared world.

I also learned how much a repeated rule can do for a world. Keeping the signs consistent gives players something they can learn through play and use again in a different place.
