BriskFit

Napses Technologies · Health and fitness algorithm research

BriskFit is a fitness coaching app where clients can follow a workout plan, log activity, and review their progress with a coach.

I researched algorithms that could help the team turn health and fitness data into measures people can understand and use.

Reviewing algorithms

I looked at ways to summarize activity and training over time. For each approach, I considered what it measures, what data it needs, and what a coach or client could learn from it.

Connecting research to the app

A calculation is useful only if the app can collect its inputs consistently and explain the result. That helped me frame the research around what a person could actually do with the information during a workout or a progress check-in.
