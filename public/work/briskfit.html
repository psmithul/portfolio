Research @ BriskFit

Napses Technologies · Health and fitness research

BriskFit is a fitness coaching program that combines workout plans, nutrition guidance, and regular check-ins.

I spoke with coaches and doctors, researched ways to measure progress, and helped develop and test our proprietary weight-loss framework.

What to measure

I spoke with fitness coaches and doctors to understand how they assess progress and what they look at when someone’s weight changes. I then reviewed algorithms for summarizing activity and training over time, looking at what data each approach needed and what a coach could learn from it.

Building the framework

I helped turn that research into a framework for BriskFit’s coaching program. My focus was the link between tracking and the next check-in. If a client logs a workout or records their weight, what can the coach learn from that information, and how might it inform a change to the plan?

Running the trials

I helped run the trials and track how participants’ weight changed over time. Our team observed an average weight loss of 6 kg per month among trial participants.

The experience taught me to start with the decision a coach needs to make, then work backward to the data and calculations that can support it.
