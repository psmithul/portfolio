Terrain-Adaptive Leaf Cleaning Module

NITK · Virtual Instrumentation team project · Jul 2026 — Present

We are developing a cleaning module that mounts beneath a collection hopper. The goal is to pick up wet and dry leaves on uneven ground while limiting how much soil enters the collector.

We completed the six-unit CAD assembly and demonstrated a printed tiller unit outdoors. Pickup and endurance testing are planned.

Keeping the pickup close to the ground

The mounting plate carries six identical tiller units. Each has a two-stage telescoping shaft, a guide collar, and a roller held in a yoke. The roller makes contact with the ground while the shaft can extend and retract vertically. This lets the pickup accommodate changes in surface height across its width.

Designing for fabrication

Repeating one tiller design keeps the number of unique parts down. We planned to print the structural parts in PETG and use purchased hardware for the rollers, springs, and rack-and-pinion lift. The design review included drawings for the single unit and the six-unit assembly. Lift motor sizing and the final rack, pinion, and spring specifications are still open.

Prototype and next tests

We checked the shaft’s vertical travel and roller contact in a CAD motion study, then demonstrated a printed unit outdoors. The next tests will measure leaf pickup and soil intake separately on wet and dry surfaces. We also plan to check whether the pickup clogs or binds over repeated runs.
