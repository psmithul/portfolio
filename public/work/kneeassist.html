Actuated Knee-Assistance System

Passive concept to actuated brace · Team project · Oct 2025 — Sep 2026

We started with a passive spring mechanism to assist knee extension. As the knee bends, the spring stretches and its line of pull changes. We used the passive design to see how those two effects shape the assistance before adding a motor.

Spring geometry

Spring force increases with stretch. The torque it applies at the knee also depends on how far its line of pull passes from the pivot. We moved the spring attachment points in SolidWorks and ANSYS to compare those effects at different angles.

We also checked clearance around the pivot and attachments through the intended range of motion. A position with useful leverage still had to leave room for the mechanism to move.

Actuated brace design

The actuated CAD uses a motor-driven cable to load the spring and an angle sensor to measure the brace position. The wearer supplies the knee motion, and the cable and spring add extension assistance. Adjustable rails position the thigh and shank cuffs around the knee, with the drive and spring mounted on one side.

Manual release

We designed a manual release with its own mechanical path. A jammed drive is one of the cases where a person would need to release the brace, so the release cannot rely on movement from the motor.

Design outcome

We completed the actuated CAD assembly and defined the drive, sensing, power, and release architecture. We presented the design at the Incubate X Prosthetic Challenge and placed in the top 5 out of 70 teams nationwide.
