Smart Traffic Light System and Transit Elevated Bus Model

Two electronic model builds · Sep 2018 — Jan 2019

I built a model traffic junction and an elevated-bus model to see how a hand-wired circuit could produce a visible sequence. I gave each model its own control circuit so I could work through the logic and wiring independently.

Traffic-light logic

I used logic gates to control the order of the lights and checked the output at each point in the sequence. The first light that came on out of order gave me a place to start tracing the wiring. That helped me debug one transition at a time.

Elevated-bus circuit

I wired a separate circuit for the bus model and checked whether it could repeat its sequence after I corrected the faults. The two builds gave me practice moving from the order I wanted, to the logic that represents it, to the connections that make it work.

Tools & methods · Logic gates · Circuit wiring · Fault tracing
