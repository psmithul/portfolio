ISTE NITK Website

ISTE NITK · Project recruitment · Aug — Sep 2026

I built a recruitment website for ISTE NITK’s roster of 40 projects across eight tracks. I wanted a prospective member to find a project they understood and see what joining it would involve.

Catalogue and project briefs

I used the submitted roster and briefs as the source material. I added browsing by track for students who had an area in mind and search by topic or tool for students who knew what they wanted to work with.

I kept the catalogue entry short. Each project has its own page, with room for the problem, work involved, and learning path when the full brief is available. That gave the catalogue and the detail page different jobs: finding a project and deciding whether to join it.

The track filter gives someone who is interested in one area a smaller set of projects to compare. I kept the same project links in the catalogue so a filtered list and the full roster both take the reader to the same brief.

Incomplete briefs

Some projects were on the roster before their briefs were complete. I gave those entries a clear status and published the information the chapter had provided. The catalogue could still show the full roster. Open the site · GitHub source.

I kept the project in the index even when the full brief was missing. That preserved its place in the right track and made it clear how much information was available. I could check the track and the project link separately from checking whether the brief itself had been published.
