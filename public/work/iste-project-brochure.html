ISTE NITK Website

ISTE NITK · Project recruitment · Aug — Sep 2026

A recruitment website where prospective members can explore 40 projects across eight ISTE NITK tracks.

Each project has a page for its brief and learning path.

What students needed from the site

The ISTE NITK recruitment roster covered eight tracks and 40 projects. I wanted someone opening it for the first time to be able to find a few projects they understood and could imagine joining.

I used the roster and the submitted briefs as the source material. The website needed to make a short overview easy to scan and leave enough space on each detail page to explain the problem and how a student could learn the work.

Organising the project roster

The site lets students browse by track or search by a topic or tool. Each result opens a brief that explains the problem, the work involved, and what a new member can learn.

I gave the catalogue two entry points. Browsing by track helps someone who already has an area in mind. Searching by topic or tool helps someone who recognizes a skill or subject without knowing how the chapter has grouped it.

Each project has a detail route. When a full brief was available, I could keep the learning path and work description together there while keeping the catalogue compact.

Keeping the index current

The catalogue follows the published project roster and can mark briefs that are still being written. Open the site · GitHub source.

I kept the roster and the brief separate enough to show an entry even when its write-up was incomplete. That let the published catalogue reflect the project list without filling gaps with guessed details.

What I learned

Working through the roster taught me to design for the decision the reader is making. A track and a short description help them decide whether to open an entry. The learning path helps them decide whether to spend time on it.

The briefs also showed me why incomplete information needs its own clear state. Some entries had enough detail to describe the work, while others could only be listed with the information the chapter had published.
