Why we printed one leaf pickup unit first

By Mithul Sourav13 November 20261 min read

Wet leaves tend to sit in dips. Set a pickup head too high and it passes over them. Set it too low and it can scrape the soil on the next raised patch. We split our pickup into six units so each could move up and down on its own.

We printed one unit in PETG and took it outside. I wanted to see how the roller met the ground and how the shaft moved as the height changed. We could check the same motion in CAD, but outside we could see the roller and the shaft move together on an actual surface.

With six units under one plate, each roller and yoke also needs room to move beside its neighbour. We checked that spacing in CAD. The outdoor demo showed us the motion of one unit, so we still had a separate question about how many leaves the whole pickup would collect.
