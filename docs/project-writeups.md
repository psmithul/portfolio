The earlier builds retain their shorter write-ups because the source materials only establish their original scope. The raw reports are not published with the site.

The journal prominently includes the GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 efficiency, and PID-versus-neural-network notes. Other published writing appears once in the archive below. Dates and sources are retained. The PID entry adds the ideal PID law and a learned one-step model with simple explanations.

Blog articles, project studies, and writing-desk previews share the Markdown renderer. It supports headings with unique anchors, image/caption figures, quotes, lists, code, tables, and KaTeX equations with MathML. Display equations use a keyboard-accessible horizontal scroll container on narrow screens. Single dollar signs remain ordinary text; use `$$` for math. The writing desk’s equation button inserts a display block. Raw HTML stays excluded and KaTeX trust stays disabled.

Owner-edited journal entries are not silently replaced by bundled Markdown. Updating an existing published seed requires an authenticated, version-checked write through the journal API.

The journal’s “Keep reading” link follows the entry order on its index and wraps only after the last entry. Entry summaries and thumbnails also open their article, with a pending indicator for slow navigation. Public metadata and article rendering share one storage read per render; new requests still read fresh publication snapshots. A scoped error page can retry a failed load. Plain entries retain the full reading column without a contents sidebar, and italic paragraphs retain body typography.
